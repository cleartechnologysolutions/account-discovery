Up to 100 per run

Checks run sequentially, roughly one every 7 seconds. A control is rechecked after each 10 addresses. Throttling or challenges stop the run.

What a result means

“Likely exists” is a sign-in discovery signal. It does not prove mailbox delivery, account access, current employment, or compromise. An email alias may differ from a sign-in name.

Data handling

Results stay in this tab until you export them. Reloading clears the workspace. Microsoft receives the candidate addresses and calibration controls. Optional internet search sends your domain and company name to Brave Search. Passwords are never sent to Microsoft.
